# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). We want everyone to be able to take part without harassment, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately:

1. Open a private report from the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/comisai/priority-axi/security/advisories/new) ("Report a vulnerability").
2. Start its title with `Conduct:`.

Only the maintainers can see these reports. You can also report a single comment to GitHub with **Report content**.

The maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, and will keep the reporter's identity confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code. Following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines), they may also issue a warning, or ban a contributor temporarily or permanently.
